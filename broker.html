Arada Broker App

Work
/
Arada Broker App

01 - Objective

To design a mobile application exclusively for external real estate brokers to:

  • Browse updated property listings
  • Check unit availability & pricing in real time
  • Book units instantly
  • Share property details with clients from the app
  • Refer or invite other brokers


02 - Background & Context

ARADA’s real estate brokers previously relied on manual documents (PDFs, WhatsApp messages, email brochures). This resulted in:

  • Outdated or missing property information
  • Delayed communication with clients
  • Errors in booking and availability
  • Heavy dependency on ARADA staff for updates

To address this, ARADA initiated a dedicated Broker App to digitize the process and empower brokers with a modern mobile-first experience.

03 - Problem Statement

“ Brokers did not have a centralized, real-time, user-friendly tool to explore and book ARADA properties. ”

Pain Points:

  • No live inventory
  • Manual processes
  • Slow booking flow
  • Difficulty sharing properties with clients
  • No unified platform for brokers

04 - Users & Stakeholders


Primary Users
  • External brokers (individual agents & broker companies)
Secondary Users
  • ARADA sales team
  • Sales coordinators
  • Admin panel users
User Needs
  • Access to updated listings instantly
  • Easy property comparison
  • Fast booking action
  • Shareable brochures & details
  • Simple onboarding
  • Ability to invite/refer brokers

05 - Research & Insights

Research Activities
  • Internal interviews with sales managers
  • Studied workflow of brokers
  • Competitive analysis of UAE real estate apps
  • Reviewed existing ARADA digital platforms
Key Insights
  • Brokers want a simple and fast system
  • Quick sharing via WhatsApp is essential
  • Booking status and availability must be LIVE
  • No complicated registration; OTP login preferred
  • Clean UI > fancy UI (they work fast and on the move)

06 - Design Goals

  • Reduce booking time from minutes to seconds
  • Provide real-time property data
  • Increase app adoption among brokers
  • Minimize dependency on ARADA staff
  • Create a clean, professional, modern UI
  • Support different broker company accounts

09 — Challenges & Solutions

Challenge Solution
Users not tech-savvy Clean minimal UI, simple flows, tutorial video
Too many property filters Implemented smart search + category chips
Manual workflows Digitized brochures, booking, availability
Multiple broker company access levels Added controlled onboarding flow

12 — Final Summary

The ARADA Broker App transformed the real estate booking workflow by giving brokers a clean, mobile-first platform with real-time access to property data. As the sole UI/UX designer, I handled the entire design cycle — research, flows, UI, prototypes, and handoff — delivering a product that improved efficiency, reduced manual work, and enhanced broker engagement.
